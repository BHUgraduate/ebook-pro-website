eBook Pro Premium

Premium costs ₹9. This page describes how it works today.

Free

₹0

  • Import and read PDF, EPUB, TXT
  • Highlights, pen annotations, flashcards
  • Translation (on-device on Android), OCR in English and Hindi, listen mode
  • Notes, Edit / Write, collections and export

Premium Premium

₹9 · paid through Razorpay

  • AI explanations, up to 20 per day, in the reader and in your notes
  • Restore purchase on the Premium screen if a payment did not register
  • Paid store books are bought one by one and are not part of Premium

Billing

Premium is bought inside the app on the Upgrade screen. Payment is handled by Razorpay and supports UPI, cards, net banking and wallets. We never see or store your card or UPI details. The app passes your email and phone number to the checkout only to pre-fill it.

Renewal and cancellation

The app currently takes a single ₹9 payment and then activates Premium on your account. It does not set up automatic renewals, so there is nothing to cancel and you are not charged again automatically. If we introduce recurring billing or an expiry, we will say so clearly before you pay and update this page and the Refund & cancellation policy.

Refunds

Purchases are generally final. Exceptions (failed activation, duplicate charge) are in the Refund & cancellation policy.

If you delete your account

Deleting your account removes your Premium status with it. See Account & data deletion.

Prices

Prices are shown in the app at checkout and are in Indian rupees. If a price on this page and the price at checkout ever differ, the checkout price applies, and please tell us.
