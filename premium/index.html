Renews every month until you cancel. Cancel any time in the app (Settings → Premium); Premium stays on until the end of the month you paid for.

Price and billing

Premium is a monthly subscription at ₹9/month. The first month is charged when you subscribe, then ₹9 every month on the same date until you cancel. It is not a one-time purchase and not lifetime access. You subscribe in the app (Settings → Premium → Start Premium). Payment is handled by Razorpay, which sets up the recurring payment through UPI Autopay, a card or another method it supports for subscriptions; you approve it once in your payment app. We never see or store your card or UPI details.

Prices are in Indian rupees. The amount shown at checkout is what you pay; your bank or payment app may show it with its own description.

Cancelling

Cancel any time in the app: Settings → Premium → Cancel subscription. Premium stays on until the end of the month you already paid for, and you are not charged again. If you revoke the mandate in your UPI app or card instead, the next renewal cannot be charged and Premium ends after the paid month.

If a renewal payment fails

Razorpay retries for a few days. Premium continues for a short grace period while it does, and the Premium screen tells you there is a payment problem. If the retries fail, the subscription stops and Premium ends.

When Premium ends

If Premium ends, nothing you made is deleted: canvases, research workspaces, summaries and notes stay on your device and still open. You cannot create new canvases or workspaces, use AI or Word export, or sync until you subscribe again; your cloud copy stays in your account.

Restore purchases

On a new phone, or if a payment did not register, open Settings → Premium → Restore purchases while signed in to the account you subscribed with. Our server re-checks your subscription with Razorpay.

Bought Premium before subscriptions?

Premium used to be a single ₹9 payment (app 1.3 and earlier). If you bought it that way, it stays active for that account and you will not be charged.
