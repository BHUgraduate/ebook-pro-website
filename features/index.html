Features

What eBook Pro can do today

Each feature is labelled with its real status. Anything not released is marked Planned and also appears on the roadmap.

Reading

A reader built around your own books, with its own typography and page-turning.

  • PDF readingFixed-layout rendering of PDF pages, with pinch-to-zoom, optional two-page spreads on wide screens, and optional automatic margin cropping.Available
  • EPUB and EPUB3 readingEPUB 2 and EPUB 3 files open as reflowable text, shown in eBook Pro’s own typography. See limitations on the formats page.Available
  • Plain text (TXT)Text files are split into paragraphs and read like any other book.Available
  • Page turningChoose a page-curl animation or a simple slide, in settings.Available
  • ZoomPinch-to-zoom and pan on PDF pages.Available
  • Search inside a bookSearch PDFs using the document’s text layer; scanned pages are read with on-device OCR on Android (English and Hindi/Devanagari).Available
  • OCR for scanned pagesScanned PDFs and book illustrations are recognised on your device with Google ML Kit, in Latin and Devanagari scripts, so you can select, copy, search and translate the text. Android only; not available in the web app.Available
  • BookmarksBookmark any page of a PDF or EPUB from the reader’s top bar. EPUB bookmarks follow the text, so they stay right when you change the font size. Bookmarks are listed in the book’s notes.Available
  • Links and footnotes in EPUBLinks inside EPUB books are tappable: a footnote opens in a pop-up, a link to another chapter jumps there with a way back, and a web link opens in your browser after asking.Available
  • Bilingual reading PremiumRead a chapter (or a PDF page) paragraph by paragraph with its translation: side by side on tablets, one under the other on phones. Each paragraph is translated once and kept, so it opens instantly next time. Android and iOS (on-device translation).Available
  • Table of contentsChapter navigation from EPUB navigation documents (EPUB 3 nav and EPUB 2 NCX) and PDF outlines, when the file includes them.Available
  • Text selectionSelect a word, a line or a passage in PDF and EPUB (in EPUB: long-press and drag). A toolbar offers Highlight, Add note, Save passage, Translate, Define/explain and Copy.Available
  • Themes and typographyThree colour themes and adjustable reading typography in settings.Available

Learning

Tools that help you understand and remember what you read.

  • AI explanations PremiumExplain a selected passage in context, powered by Google’s Gemini and run through eBook Pro’s own server. Premium only, up to 20 requests per day.Available
  • AI summaries PremiumSummaries of the current page, chapter or whole book. A book is never sent whole: the summary uses the contents with your saved chapter summaries or each chapter’s opening, and says so. Summaries are saved with their source, so they open again offline. A whole-book summary counts as 3 of the 20 daily AI uses.Available
  • TranslationTranslate selected text between English, Hindi and about 28 other languages. The source language is detected automatically. On Android it runs on your device (Google ML Kit; language packs download once, then work offline); in the web app the text is sent to an online translation service.Available
  • Hindi and Unicode supportHindi (Devanagari) text is shown, selected, searched, translated, saved in notes and exported correctly, using bundled Noto fonts so it looks the same on every device.Available
  • Listen (read aloud)Text-to-speech using the voices built into your device.Available
  • Dictionary pop-upsLong-press a word for a definition. The bundled offline dictionary is currently a small sample; Wikipedia summaries cover more.Partial
  • AI: explain, simplify, summarize, examplesPremium readers can ask for an explanation, a simpler rewrite, a short summary or examples of a selected passage. AI answers are labelled as generated and can be saved as a note or flashcard.Available
  • Wikipedia lookupFetch a short Wikipedia summary, thumbnail and link for a selected word or phrase, in English or Hindi (hi.wikipedia.org). Requires internet.Available
  • Highlights and pen annotationsDraw with a pen or highlighter directly on the page. A smart highlighter can snap to the line of text.Available
  • FlashcardsTurn a highlight into a flashcard and review with spaced repetition (SM-2). Cards are currently generated by blanking the highlighted text in its sentence (cloze); AI-written cards are planned.Available
  • Community highlightsOptionally see which passages other readers highlighted in store books. Only anonymous counts of highlighted ranges are shared.Available

Personal library

Your books, on your device.

  • Import your own booksImport PDF, EPUB and TXT files from your device. Imported files stay on your device.Available
  • Reading progressPage, percentage, pages left in chapter and an estimated time remaining, saved on your device.Available
  • Book storeBrowse a catalogue of books, with search across public sources. Downloading store books requires an account.Available
  • Ratings and reviewsSigned-in readers can review store books.Available
  • Import web articlesPaste a web address to save an article (text, images, title, author and date) as a book in your library, with a link back to the original. Read, highlight, search and cite it offline. Pages that need a sign-in or build their text with JavaScript cannot be imported, and the app says so.Available
  • Cloud sync PremiumOptional (Settings → Sync, signed in): your notes, highlights, bookmarks, flashcards, dictionary, collections, saved passages, canvases, research workspaces and voice notes stay in step across your devices, offline first. If the same note is changed on two devices, both versions are kept. Book files, reading positions and pen marks stay on each device.Available

Notes and writing

One notes library shared by the reader and the Edit / Write workspace. Notes stay on your device unless you turn on Sync.

  • Edit / Write workspaceA rich-text editor with headings, lists, checklists, quotes, code, tables, images, handwriting, six paper styles, autosave, undo/redo and find and replace. Open it from the bookshelf menu (landscape).Available
  • Notes linked to your booksTurn a selection into a highlight or a note. Notes remember the exact place in the PDF or EPUB, show as markers on the page, and take you back with one tap.Available
  • Search everythingOne search box (Ctrl or Cmd + K on a keyboard, or the search button on Home and Library) finds books, notes, highlights, flashcards and your dictionary. Every result shows where it came from and opens the exact page in the book. It also runs commands such as New note or Your Cards.Available
  • Voice notesRecord a voice note about a page or attach one to a note or highlight; pause and resume, play it back with its waveform, name it, add a transcript, share it as an audio file. Android and iOS. (Automatic transcription is not available yet.)Available
  • Knowledge canvas PremiumA board where notes, highlights, saved passages, AI answers, voice notes, books and collections become cards you can drag, colour and connect, with labelled links. Each card opens its object or the exact place in the book it came from.Available
  • Research workspaces PremiumStudy several books and articles together: a research question, the documents, everything you marked in them, search across their full text, a side-by-side comparison, pinned passages, and (Premium) AI answers that cite numbered sources. Export with citations as Word or Markdown.Available
  • Collections that hold anythingA collection can hold notes, highlights, cards, words, passages and voice notes as well as books, in your own order. Export a collection as Word or Markdown.Available
  • Word export PremiumExport notes, a folder of notes, a collection or a research workspace as a real Word document (.docx) with headings, lists, tables, images, links and citations, Hindi included.Available
  • My DictionarySave a word or phrase while reading, with its meaning, an example sentence, your own note and the page you met it on. Search, edit and export your words, or turn one into a flashcard.Available
  • Your CardsAll your flashcards in one place: review everything that is due across every book, or open one deck. Each card can reopen the page it came from. Spaced repetition with Again, Hard, Good and Easy.Available
  • Keep what you look upFrom the selection card, save a translation, an AI answer or a Wikipedia summary as a note, make a flashcard from it, or keep the word. Each is saved with the book and page it came from, and the card keeps the book’s text, the translation and AI output visibly separate.Available
  • Smart collectionsCollections that fill themselves: Currently reading, Not started, Unfinished, Finished, Recently opened, Recently annotated, 10+ highlights and With notes.Available
  • Reading dashboardReading time this week, books in progress and finished, notes, highlights, dictionary words and flashcards due. Counts only, no points or badges.Available
  • Export your dataExport flashcards and dictionary words as CSV (opens in Anki, Excel, Numbers and Google Sheets, Hindi included), and notes as TXT, Markdown, PDF or EPUB.Available
  • Wishlist and sharingHeart a store book to keep it on your wishlist, synced to your account. Share a book with a link that opens it in the app, or sends your friend to the download.Available
  • Collections, tags and foldersGroup books into collections; organise notes with folders, tags, favourites and pins; search across all notes, including Hindi.Available
  • ExportExport notes as TXT, Markdown, PDF or EPUB. Hindi notes are exported with correct letter shaping and fonts.Available

Productivity

Habits and focus.

  • Focus modeA Pomodoro-style timer (25/5 by default) that dims reading controls while you work.Available
  • Reading streaksA daily streak that counts reading time against a threshold (15 minutes by default).Available
  • Reading statisticsTime spent reading and speed estimates, kept on your device.Available
