Privacy considerations for AI

When you request an explanation, eBook Pro sends the selected text, the surrounding paragraph, the target language and your sign-in token to our server, which forwards the text to Google’s Gemini API. Avoid selecting passages that contain sensitive personal information. Details: AI and Privacy Policy.
