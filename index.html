Reading experience

Made for long-form reading

Page-curl or slide page turning, three colour themes and adjustable typography. PDFs keep their original layout with zoom and search; EPUB and TXT re-flow to fit your screen. A focus timer and daily streak help you keep going.

AI-powered learning

Understand what you read

Long-press a word for a definition, a Wikipedia summary, an on-device translation or to hear it read aloud. Premium readers can also ask Google’s Gemini to explain a passage in context. Today that is the only AI feature, and it is limited to 20 requests a day.

Personal library

Your library, on your device

Imported books, reading progress, highlights, flashcards and streaks are stored on the device or browser you use. An optional account adds a store where you can download books and leave reviews.

Cross-device

Honest about sync

Your account and store library are stored online, but imported books and reading data do not sync between devices yet. We list it on the roadmap as something we are exploring, not as a feature you have today.

Common questions

What is eBook Pro?

eBook Pro is a reading and learning app for PDF, EPUB and text books. You can import your own books, highlight and annotate, look words up, translate, listen, make flashcards, and (with Premium) ask for AI explanations of passages.

Is eBook Pro free?

Reading your own books, highlighting, flashcards, translation, listen mode, focus mode and streaks are free. AI explanations and access to Premium store content need Premium (₹9). See Premium.

Is an account required?

No, not to read. The bookshelf works signed out and your imported books stay on your device. An account is needed to download store books, post reviews, use AI explanations and buy Premium.

Can I import my own books?

Yes. Import PDF, EPUB and TXT files from your device. Imported files stay on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

What formats are supported?

PDF, EPUB (2 and 3) and TXT. MOBI, DOCX and DRM-protected files are not supported. Details and limitations are on the formats page.
