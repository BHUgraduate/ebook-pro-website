Free and Premium

Reading stays free. Premium is ₹9/month, renews monthly until you cancel, and unlocks the knowledge and research tools.

Free

A complete reader, always

  • Reading: PDF, EPUB, EPUB 3 and TXT, themes, typography, page turning, contents, bookmarks, read aloud
  • Your library: Import books and web articles, collections of anything, smart collections, reading progress and goals
  • Highlights and notes: Select, highlight, note, save passages, pen marks on PDFs, Edit / Write, Markdown and PDF export
  • Search: Search inside a book and across everything you saved
  • OCR: Scanned pages and images, English and Hindi, on your device (Android)
  • Translation and dictionary: On-device translation of selections, Wikipedia, My Dictionary
  • Study: Flashcards with spaced repetition, Your Cards, reading dashboard
  • Voice notes: Record, play, name and share voice notes on this device

Premium · ₹9/month

Everything in Free, plus

  • AI explanations: Explain, simplify, summarise or give examples for any passage, and translate Sanskrit, with up to 20 AI requests a day.
  • AI summaries: Summaries of a page, a chapter or a whole book, saved with their source so they open again offline.
  • Knowledge canvas: Lay out notes, highlights, passages, AI answers, voice notes and books as cards, connect them with labelled links, and open any card at its source.
  • Research workspaces: Study several books and articles together: search across all of them, compare passages side by side, pin evidence and ask AI questions answered with citations.
  • Bilingual reading: Read a chapter or a PDF page paragraph by paragraph next to its translation, side by side on tablets.
  • Word export: Export notes, collections and research workspaces as Word documents (.docx) with headings, tables, images and citations.
  • Cloud sync: Keep notes, highlights, cards, words, collections, canvases, workspaces and voice notes in step across your devices, with 500 MB of cloud storage for your files.

Renews every month until you cancel. Cancel any time in the app (Settings → Premium); Premium stays on until the end of the month you paid for.

If Premium ends, nothing you made is deleted: canvases, research workspaces, summaries and notes stay on your device and still open. You cannot create new canvases or workspaces, use AI or Word export, or sync until you subscribe again; your cloud copy stays in your account. Paid store books are bought one by one and are not part of Premium. Premium details →

Common questions

What is eBook Pro?

eBook Pro is a reader for your PDF, EPUB and text books that turns what you read into knowledge: highlights and notes that remember their source, OCR, translation, flashcards and a dictionary for free, and with Premium AI explanations and summaries, a knowledge canvas, research workspaces, bilingual reading, Word export and cloud sync.

Is eBook Pro free?

Yes, as a reader: reading, your library, highlights, notes, bookmarks, OCR, translation, the dictionary, flashcards, voice notes and Edit / Write are free. Premium costs ₹9/month and renews monthly until you cancel; it adds AI, summaries, the knowledge canvas, research workspaces, bilingual reading, Word export and sync. See Premium.

Is an account required?

No, not to read. The bookshelf works signed out and your imported books stay on your device. An account is needed to download store books, post reviews, use AI explanations and buy Premium.

Can I import my own books?

Yes. Import PDF, EPUB and TXT files from your device. Imported files stay on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

What formats are supported?

PDF, EPUB (2 and 3) and TXT. MOBI, DOCX and DRM-protected files are not supported. Details and limitations are on the formats page.

Does it support PDF?

Yes, with zoom, search, text selection, a table of contents when the PDF has an outline, and pen/highlighter annotations.
