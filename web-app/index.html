The eBook Pro web app

The browser-based version of eBook Pro, at ebookpro.web.app.

Launch web app

What it is

The same reader and tools as the app, running in your browser. Import PDF, EPUB and TXT files, read, highlight, and sign in for store features.

What to know

  • Imported books and reading data are stored in your browser. Clearing site data removes them, and they do not follow you to other browsers or devices.
  • OCR (scanned pages) and on-device translation are available in the Android app only; the web app translates through an online service.
  • AI explanations need a Premium account and an internet connection.
