Refund & Cancellation Policy

How Premium payments, cancellation and refunds work.

Last updated: 11 October 2026

How Premium is paid

Premium (₹9) is bought in the app and processed by Razorpay. The app takes one payment and then activates Premium on your account. It does not currently set up automatic renewals.

Cancellation

Because there is no recurring charge today, there is nothing to cancel and you will not be billed again automatically. If we introduce recurring billing or an expiry date in future, we will show it clearly before you pay and update this policy first.

Refunds

Premium gives immediate access to AI features and premium content that use paid cloud services, so purchases are generally final and not refundable, and we do not give credits for partly used periods.

We will, however, help or refund you where:

  • you were charged but Premium was not activated and we cannot fix it;
  • you were charged more than once for the same purchase;
  • a technical fault on our side prevented you from using what you paid for and we cannot resolve it; or
  • the law where you live gives you a right to a refund.

How to ask

Email support.ebookpro@gmail.com within 7 days of the payment with the Razorpay payment ID, the date, and the email or phone used. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method; timing depends on your bank or payment provider.

Deleting your account

Deleting your account ends your Premium access and does not by itself create a refund.
