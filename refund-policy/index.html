Refund & Cancellation Policy

How Premium payments, cancellation and refunds work.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

How Premium is paid

Premium is a subscription at ₹9/month, processed by Razorpay. The first month is charged when you subscribe, then ₹9 every month until you cancel. Paid store books are separate one-time purchases.

Cancellation

Cancel any time in the app: Settings → Premium → Cancel subscription. You are not charged again; Premium stays on until the end of the month you already paid for. Cancelling does not by itself refund the current month. If you revoke the mandate in your UPI app or card instead, the next renewal cannot be charged and Premium ends after the paid month.

Refunds

Premium gives immediate access to AI features that use paid cloud services, so monthly payments are generally not refundable, and we do not give credits for partly used months.

We will, however, help or refund you where:

  • you were charged but Premium was not activated and we cannot fix it;
  • you were charged more than once for the same month or purchase;
  • you were charged after cancelling;
  • a technical fault on our side prevented you from using what you paid for and we cannot resolve it; or
  • the law where you live gives you a right to a refund.

How to ask

Email support.ebookpro@gmail.com within 7 days of the payment with the Razorpay payment ID, the date, and the email or phone used. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method; timing depends on your bank or payment provider.

Deleting your account

Deleting your account cancels the subscription’s renewal and ends your Premium access; it does not by itself create a refund.
