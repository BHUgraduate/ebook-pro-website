1. Who we are and what this covers

eBook Pro is a reading and learning app developed by Hycyon, Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh, India (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the eBook Pro Android app (package com.hycyon.ebookpro), the web app at ebookpro.web.app, and this website. It describes what the apps actually do today. Contact: support.ebookpro@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • You can read without an account. Books you import, your reading progress, highlights, notes, flashcards and streaks stay on your device (or in your browser for the web app). We do not upload them.
  • If you create an account, we store your account details and a few account-related records online (see section 3).
  • If you use AI explanations (Premium), the text you select and its surrounding paragraph are sent through our server to Google’s Gemini.
  • On Android, translation and OCR run on your device. In the web app, translation is performed by an online translation service and the text you translate is sent to it (see sections 6 and 7).
  • We do not sell your personal information. The app does not include an advertising SDK or a dedicated analytics or crash-reporting SDK.
  • You can delete your account in the app or at Account & data deletion.

4. Data that stays on your device

The following are saved only on your device (in your browser for the web app) and are not sent to us: imported book files, reading progress and position, highlights and pen annotations, notes and handwriting you write (including exported files, which you save or share yourself), text recognised from scanned pages (OCR), flashcards and study schedule, reading streaks and statistics, and app settings. Uninstalling the app or clearing browser data removes them. We cannot recover them for you.

5. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account, sign you in and let you recover access.
  • To provide features you ask for: store downloads, reviews, community highlights, AI explanations, Premium.
  • To enforce usage limits and prevent abuse.
  • To process and support Premium purchases.
  • To respond to your support requests.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

6. AI, translation and OCR

AI explanations are available to Premium accounts. When you request one, the app sends the selected text, its surrounding paragraph, the output language and your sign-in token over HTTPS to a Google Cloud function that we operate. The function verifies your account and Premium status, counts the request against your daily limit (20 per day), and sends the text to a Google Gemini model to produce the explanation. The Gemini API key is held on our server, not in the app.

To avoid repeating identical requests, the function caches the answer in Firestore under a one-way hash of the text, context and language. The cache holds the answer text and a timestamp, not your account ID. Google handles data sent to the Gemini API under its own terms; please review Google’s documentation. Please do not select sensitive personal information. See also AI.

Translation. In the Android app, translation runs on your device with Google ML Kit; the selected text is not sent to us or to a translation server. The first time you use a language, its model is downloaded from Google. In the web app, on-device models are not available, so when you tap Translate the selected text is sent over HTTPS to the MyMemory translation service (api.mymemory.translated.net), which returns the translation. It is not sent to our servers.

OCR (reading text from scanned pages and images, including Hindi/Devanagari) and read-aloud run on your device and do not send your pages or text to us.

8. What other people can see

  • Your display name, username and profile photo appear next to reviews you post, from a separate public profile record. Your email address, phone number, wishlist and Premium status are private to your account.
  • Your profile photo is stored at a web address that is publicly readable if someone has the link.
  • Community highlights show anonymous counts only.

9. Security

We use industry-standard services and safeguards, described on our Security page: HTTPS for network traffic, Firebase Authentication, access rules on our database and storage, server-side verification of Premium status, and keeping API secrets on the server. No online service can promise absolute security.

10. Retention and deletion

While your account exists we keep the records in section 3. When you delete your account (in the app, or at Account & data deletion), our server permanently deletes: your profile and everything under it (store library, wishlist, AI counters, settings), your public profile, your username record, any pending password-reset code, your reviews, your profile photo, and your sign-in account. Reports you filed against others are kept for moderation but no longer carry your identity.

What may remain: payment records held by Razorpay and by banks, which are kept under financial and legal rules; the minimal payment record our server keeps for each verified payment (payment and order ID, amount, what was bought and your account ID), kept for accounting, refunds and legal reasons; anonymous community-highlight counts; cached AI answers that are not linked to you; and short-lived technical logs and residual copies that Google Cloud may keep for a limited period under its own policies. We do not intentionally keep separate long-term copies of your account data. Data on your device is removed by uninstalling the app or clearing browser data.

11. Your choices and rights

You can use eBook Pro without an account, edit your name, photo and recovery details in My Account, turn community highlights and AI off in settings, and delete your account at any time. You may ask us for access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by emailing support.ebookpro@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and may need to verify that the request comes from you.

12. Children

eBook Pro is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Where data is processed

Google Firebase and Google Cloud process data on servers that may be outside your country, including in the United States. Our AI function runs in Google Cloud’s us-central1 region. By using online features you understand your data may be transferred there.

14. This website

This website does not use cookies, advertising or analytics scripts, and its fonts are served from the site itself. Like any web host, the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may log standard request data such as IP address. The account deletion page loads Google’s Firebase scripts so you can sign in to your account.

15. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app or on this site. Earlier versions are available on request.
