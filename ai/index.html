AI

How AI works in eBook Pro

One AI feature is released today. This page explains what it does, who can use it, and where your text goes.

Available now

AI explanations Premium

Select a passage and ask for an explanation. eBook Pro sends the selected text and its surrounding paragraph to a Google Cloud function that we run. That function verifies your account and Premium status, applies your daily limit, and asks a Google Gemini model to explain the passage concisely in the language you choose. The answer streams back to the app.

Runs on your device (not AI requests to us)

  • Translation: Google ML Kit, on-device on Android (models download on demand); MyMemory online service in the web app.
  • OCR: Google ML Kit, on-device (Android), Latin and Devanagari.
  • Listen (read aloud): your device’s text-to-speech voices.
  • Dictionary: a small bundled dictionary; Wikipedia lookups call Wikipedia.

Limits

PlanAI explanations
Signed outNot available

How requests are handled

  • Sent over HTTPS with your sign-in token, which our server verifies.
  • The text is forwarded to Google’s Gemini API, using a key that is stored on our server and never shipped in the app.
  • A shared cache stores answers keyed by a one-way hash of the text, context and language, so identical requests can be answered without calling Gemini again. The cache stores the answer, not your account ID.
  • Your account stores only a per-day counter of AI requests, for the limit.
  • Google processes the text under its own terms for the Gemini API. See Google’s documentation for how Google handles API data.

What not to submit

Do not select passages that contain passwords, financial or health details, government IDs, or other sensitive personal information, and avoid material you are not allowed to share. AI answers can be wrong; verify anything important.
