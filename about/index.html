About eBook Pro

A reading app that helps you understand what you read.

What it is

eBook Pro is a reading and learning app for PDF, EPUB and text books. It combines a calm, consistent reader with lookups, translation, listening, highlights, flashcards and, for Premium readers, AI explanations.

Why it exists

Reading serious material means stopping to look things up. eBook Pro aims to keep you on the page: understand a word or passage, capture it, and remember it later.

Principles

  • Your books stay yours. Imported files stay on your device.
  • Honest about what is built. Planned work is labelled as planned.
  • AI where it helps. A study aid, clearly labelled, with limits we state.

Who makes it
