Formats

Supported formats

eBook Pro opens PDF, EPUB (2 and 3) and TXT files. This page states exactly what works for each, including limitations.

PDF

Fixed-layout pages rendered with the PDFium engine.

PDF capabilities
CapabilitySupportNotes
Import✔ YesFrom device storage, copied into the app.
Reading✔ YesPage-by-page; one page on phones, optionally two-page spreads on wide screens.
Search✔ YesUses the PDF’s own text layer. Pages without text are read with on-device OCR (Android and iOS only).
Text extraction✔ YesUsed for selection, lookups and search. Scanned PDFs depend on OCR quality.
Images✔ YesPages are rendered as images, so figures appear as in the original.
Layout✔ YesOriginal layout preserved. Optional automatic margin cropping.
Navigation◐ PartialPDF outline (bookmarks) is used when the file contains one.
Zoom✔ YesPinch-to-zoom and pan.
Highlights & annotations✔ YesPen and highlighter drawn over the page.

EPUB (EPUB 2 and EPUB 3)

Packages are unpacked and re-flowed as text in eBook Pro’s typography.

EPUB (EPUB 2 and EPUB 3) capabilities
CapabilitySupportNotes
Import✔ YesEPUB 2 and EPUB 3 packages are recognised.
Reading✔ YesRe-flowed text with your chosen theme and typography.
Search◐ PartialBasic text search; verify results on large books.
Text extraction✔ YesText is extracted directly from the book.
Images◐ PartialImage-only fixed-layout EPUBs (comics, picture books) open as page images. Images inside reflowable chapters are not displayed currently.
Layout◐ PartialThe book’s own CSS is not applied; eBook Pro’s typography is used instead. Fixed-layout books that contain text are shown as reflowable text.
Navigation✔ YesEPUB 3 navigation document and EPUB 2 NCX are both read.
Zoom◐ PartialAdjust text size and typography; pinch-zoom applies to PDF and image-page books.
Highlights & annotations✔ YesPen and highlighter, and flashcards from highlights.

TXT

Plain text, split into paragraphs.

TXT capabilities
CapabilitySupportNotes
Import✔ YesPlain text files.
Reading✔ YesRe-flowed text.
Search◐ PartialBasic text search.
Text extraction✔ YesThe file is already text.
Images— NoNot applicable.
Layout✔ YesParagraphs are split on blank lines.
Navigation◐ PartialNo chapters; progress by page and percentage.
Zoom◐ PartialAdjust text size and typography.
Highlights & annotations✔ YesPen and highlighter.

Not supported

  • MOBI / AZW
  • DOCX
  • CBZ / CBR comic archives
  • DRM-protected files
OCR

OCR is used to search scanned PDF pages. It runs on your device (Google ML Kit, Latin script) on Android and iOS, and is not available in the web app.
