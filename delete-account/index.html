Account

Delete your eBook Pro account

Permanently delete your account and the data we hold about you. You can do it here or in the app.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account
  • Your profile, display name, recovery email and phone
  • Your store library, settings and AI usage counters
  • Your username record
  • Your reviews and profile photo
  • Premium status (no automatic refund)

What may remain

  • Payment records held by Razorpay and banks
  • Anonymous community-highlight counts and cached AI answers not linked to you
  • Moderation reports you filed, without your identity
  • Short-lived technical logs held by Google Cloud
Deletion is permanent

It cannot be undone. Books on your device are not affected until you uninstall the app or clear your browser data. See the Privacy Policy.

Delete in the app

Settings → My Account → Delete My Account.
