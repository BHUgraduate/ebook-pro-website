Security

Security at eBook Pro

What we actually do to protect your account and data, without exaggeration.

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Authentication

Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication (email and password, phone one-time code, username and password, or Google). We never see or store your password. Our server checks your sign-in token on every AI request. Password-reset codes for username accounts expire after 15 minutes.

Secure communication

The apps talk to our backend and to the third-party services listed in the Privacy Policy over HTTPS.

Cloud data and access control

Account data lives in Firebase (Firestore and Storage), protected by security rules that restrict who can read and write. Your Premium status can only be set by our server after a verified payment, not by the app. Payment checkout orders are created on our server, and payment confirmations are verified by signature before Premium is activated.

Secrets

The Gemini API key and payment secret keys are kept on the server, never in the app or this website.

AI data handling

AI requests are sent to Google’s Gemini through our server, as described on the AI page.

Data on your device

Books and reading data are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. Protect your device with a screen lock.

Data deletion

You can delete your account and server-side data at any time: Account & data deletion.

Limits

We rely on Google’s infrastructure and its default protections, such as encryption in transit and at rest as documented by Google. We have not had an independent security certification, and no service can guarantee perfect security.

Reporting a security issue

Email support.ebookpro@gmail.com with the subject “Security report”. Please give us reasonable time to fix an issue before disclosing it, and do not access other people’s data while testing.
